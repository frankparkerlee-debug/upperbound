Logo studies.

Five directions for the Upperbound mark. Each shown as the lockup, the mark alone on brand grounds, and at app-icon size. All draw from the same idea: the ceiling is a variable, and the mark should carry the ceiling.

A · The Overbar

What the site already wears, formalized. The bar above the wordmark is the upper bound itself: present, but yours to move. Quietest of the five; the wordmark stays the hero.

B · The Ceiling

In mathematics, ⌈x⌉ is the ceiling operator, the literal notation for an upper bound. Wrapping the u in ceiling brackets makes the name a symbol. Ownable, smart without explaining itself, and no competitor can wear it.

C · The Break

The ceiling line with a gap where the u rises through it. The mark tells the whole brand story in one gesture: there was a limit, and you went past it. The most narrative of the five.

D · The Coin

The overbar u sealed in a racing-green circle. Not a new idea, a packaging of A: instantly an app icon, a favicon, an embroidered chest mark, a pill-bottle-free brand seal for the welcome kit.

E · The Ascender

A u whose right stem keeps going, rising past where the letter should stop and carrying the bar up with it. The letter itself breaks its own ceiling. Most distinctive as a drawn glyph; needs a type designer's hand to finish properly.

My read: B (The Ceiling) is the most ownable idea and D (The Coin) is the most immediately useful packaging of what exists. B for the brand mark, D's coin construction as its app-icon vessel, would cover every surface.
