Questions

The fine print, in plain type.

Is this a subscription?

It's a 12-week arc billed monthly. Your billing is set to end when the arc ends, so nothing renews unless you actively choose another arc. You can cancel from your dashboard in two clicks at any time.

What's included in the price?

Everything: the physician review, your personalized pathway, what's prescribed if you're approved, shipping, the week-six check-in, and the week-twelve reassessment. There are no add-on fees.

What's actually prescribed?

It depends on your pathway and your physician's judgment. Weight Loss pathways are built on GLP-1 class therapy, Output on NAD+, and Recovery on sermorelin. Your physician prescribes only if it's appropriate for you, adjusts at your check-ins, and if it isn't right for you, declines your intake and you're refunded in full. We publish no dosing information; that conversation belongs with your physician.

Who reviews my intake?

A licensed physician, personally, within 24 hours. If a pathway isn't appropriate for you, they decline it and your payment is refunded in full.

Do I need blood work or a lab visit?

No. Your intake is completed online and reviewed by a physician. Because we don't test, we will never claim a lab marker moved. If your physician believes you need testing before it's safe to proceed, they'll tell you that instead of approving you.

What if I'm not approved?

You pay nothing. Your payment is refunded in full and you'll see the physician's reasoning in your dashboard.

Where is Upperbound available?

We're launching in Texas first, with fulfillment through a licensed Texas pharmacy. More states follow as our physician and pharmacy network expands.
