06

Asked, answered.

The fine print, out loud
Is this legitimate medicine?
Yes — and verifiably. Licensed physicians in every state we serve, an accredited partner pharmacy dispensing under their own label, and a clinic built to the standard LegitScript certification requires. If a therapy isn't cleared under current law, it isn't offered here.
Will I definitely be prescribed something?
No. Treatment happens only where a licensed physician determines it's appropriate after reviewing your full intake. Sometimes the honest answer is a plan without a prescription — and if we're not right for you at all, you don't get charged. You get told.
What happens at a checkpoint?
At week six and week twelve you report back, and your physician holds, adjusts, or stops the plan. Nothing renews on autopilot — a pathway continues because you both decide it should.
What does the monthly number include?
Everything on the pathway: physician care, your plan, both checkpoints, unlimited messaging, shipping — and treatment, if your physician prescribes it. No membership stacked on top, no surprise fees at checkout.
Can I really cancel in two clicks?
Yes — from your account page, no phone call, no retention script. We'd rather earn twelve months than trap one.
